FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which documents are needed?+

The Directorate provides the sale agreement, down-payment certificate, cadastre and valuation report. You provide an application, incorporation documents, consent to borrow and a cash-flow forecast — bank staff help with most of them.

Can I buy a unit at auction?+

Yes. Directorate units are also sold through an electronic online auction, and the bank accepts the winner’s protocol instead of a sale agreement.

I want to buy wholesale — who do I talk to?+

Choose “Wholesale purchase” in the form. The Directorate will connect you with the right manufacturers.

Can I buy single items?+

Yes, you can browse and buy individual items at the zone’s trading outlets.
